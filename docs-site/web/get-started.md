# Web Get Started

Use this sequence when you are setting up a new Nexio account or cleaning up an existing one.

## Before you begin
- Open the Nexio web portal in a browser.
- Make sure you have the account credentials you want to use.
- Have service keys, device codes, or provider sign-in details ready for the integrations you plan to connect.

## Recommended setup order
1. Sign in and confirm you are in the right account.
2. Add or verify your core providers in [Addon Manager](./admin-workspaces/addons.md).
3. Organize discovery in [Catalog Inventory](./admin-workspaces/catalogs.md).
4. Connect services in [Integrations](./admin-workspaces/integrations.md).
5. Set the stream card style in [Formatter Getting Started](./admin-workspaces/formatter-getting-started.md).

## Why this order works
- Addons determine what content and stream sources are available.
- Catalogs decide what shows up first and what stays hidden.
- Integrations enrich the account with identity, metadata, and provider features.
- The formatter works best when the underlying stream data is already stable.

## After each change
- Click the save or sync action in the current workspace.
- Revisit the Android app or the portal preview to confirm the change looks the way you expect.
- If something looks off, fix the earliest layer that could cause it rather than layering on more changes.

## Good first outcomes
- One working addon is installed.
- Home rails are ordered the way you want.
- Required services are connected and showing the right account state.
- Stream cards are readable and consistent.
