# Account

This bridge page points to the new Start Here account guidance.

## Portal-specific context
Use the website to prepare the account before you sign in on the TV app. The portal session stays in your browser, but the account state you save is meant to follow the account rather than one device.

## Where this matters
- Confirm the account identity shown in the portal before changing addons, integrations, or catalogs.
- Use the portal when you want to adjust account-scoped setup.
- Use the TV app QR flow when you want to watch on a device.
