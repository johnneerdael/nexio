# Account and Sign In

## What this helps with
This page covers where account setup belongs, how QR sign-in fits into the TV flow, and how to confirm you are using the right Nexio account.

## Where you do this
Use the website to prepare the account. Use the TV app to sign in with QR when you are ready to watch, and use the TV app settings only to confirm or change the active account on that device.

## Recommended setup
1. Prepare addons, integrations, and catalog views on the website first.
2. Sign in on the TV app with QR.
3. Confirm the account shown on TV matches the one you configured on the website.
4. Return to the website whenever you need to adjust the account setup itself.
