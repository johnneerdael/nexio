# Start Here

Nexio is watched in the TV app. The website is the easiest place to prepare setup before you open the app, especially when you are wiring addons, integrations, or catalog views for the first time.

## Recommended path

- [Recommended Setup](./recommended-setup.md) - prepare the account on the website before you switch to the TV app.
- [First-run Sync and Cache](./first-run-sync-and-cache.md) - understand why Home fills in over time and what the disk-backed cache changes later.
- [Account and Sign In](./account-and-sign-in.md) - check the sign-in flow and account-scoped behavior.
- [Security and Data](./security-and-data.md) - review how Nexio handles account state and credentials.

## Fastest way through

1. Prepare addons, integrations, and catalog views on the website.
2. Sign in on the TV app with QR.
3. Wait for Trakt and catalog sync.
4. Let the disk-backed cache warm up before deciding setup is complete.

## After setup

- Go to [Watching](/watch/) when you are ready to understand Home, Detail, trailers, and the normal day-to-day flow.
- Go to [Playback](/playback/) when the app is working and you want better buffering, subtitle, or device guidance.

## Need help

If setup does not look right, read [First-run Sync and Cache](./first-run-sync-and-cache.md) first, then move to [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting/) if it still looks broken.
